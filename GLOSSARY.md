# Leadline

A personal outreach machine for one founder's job search: it decides who to
contact, gives them the reason, and remembers when to try again.

## Language

**Company**:
An organization worth contacting, as distinct from any person inside it. Holds
what is publicly knowable — headcount, funding round, open roles, location —
and carries no outreach state of its own.
_Avoid_: Account, target, org, record

**Lead**:
One named person at a Company, plus the pitch you would make to them. The unit
you actually work, track and follow up on. Two people at the same Company are
two Leads.
_Avoid_: Company, contact, prospect, record

**Stage**:
How far along a Company is as a business — seed, Series A, profitable. A fact
about them, never something you change.
_Avoid_: Status, round

**Status**:
Where a Lead stands with you — sourced, sent, replied, call, revisit, ghosted.
Something only you change, by touching it.
_Avoid_: Stage, state

**Attempt**:
One try at reaching a Lead: a DM, an email, a follow-up. Counted when deciding
whether someone has gone cold.
_Avoid_: Touch, send, ping, contact (as a noun), interaction

**Brief**:
The remembered case for writing to one Lead — who they are, why now, why you,
what you are offering. Read by the writer, never by the Lead.
_Avoid_: Draft, template, script, prompt

**Ritual**:
The daily session the tool exists to serve: pick who is due, read why they
matter, write the message yourself, record that you sent it.
_Avoid_: Workflow, session, sprint

**Revisit**:
A Lead who said "not now". Not a rejection and not closed — timed, with a date
to come back.
_Avoid_: Rejected, deferred, maybe, archive

**Proof link**:
The single artifact a message leads with, so the message carries evidence
rather than a claim.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, sample, attachment

**Dossier**:
The regenerated Markdown summary of one Company — facts, hiring signal, people,
provenance — written for a human or an agent to read offline.
_Avoid_: Report, profile, page

**Verified**:
A contact detail a human has confirmed reaches the person. Inference, pattern
guessing, and "it looks right" are not verification.
_Avoid_: Valid, found, scraped
